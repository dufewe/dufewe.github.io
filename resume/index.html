About Me

Currently, I am one Ph.D. candidate in Particle Physics at Central China Normal University. My research lies at the intersection of flavor physics, effective field theory, and phenomenology beyond the Standard Model. I work on semi-leptonic B decays, flavor anomalies, and cosmological tensions — probing new physics through precision theoretical calculations and phenomenological analyses.

Beyond theory, I am passionate about bringing modern AI into high energy physics — from accelerating theoretical computations to discovering hidden patterns in experimental data. My goal is to bridge traditional phenomenology with AI-driven methods, building tools that can help uncover what the Standard Model leaves unanswered.
