Hello, Blog

2026-11-26 Site

Every personal site starts as a folder with good intentions. This one has been a cover page and a CV for a while; now it gets a place for words that are too long for a caption and too short for a paper.

What will live here

  • Research notes — how a calculation actually went, not just the final formula;
  • Tool notes — the small scripts, checks and habits that make the work faster;
  • Reading notes — papers worth a second pass, and why.

The bar is simple: if it will still be useful in six months, it belongs here. Hot takes can stay in the group chat.

How it is built

Plain HTML and CSS, a little JavaScript, and GitHub Pages. No build step, no framework, nothing to upgrade at 2 a.m. The background rotates through a small list of images once per session; everything else is static files you can read in a text editor.

There is only one heroism in the world: to see the world as it is, and to love it. — Romain Rolland

That quote sits on the home page, and it is as good a reason as any to keep writing. First real posts are on their way.
